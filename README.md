# Retail-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales and profit performance across different categories, regions, customer segments, and time periods.
An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to help understand business performance and identify area for improvement.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Analysis Performance 
- Analyzed total Sales, Profit, and Quantity
- Compared sales and profit across product categories
- Analyzed regional performance
- Compared different customer segments
- Analyzed monthly sales and profit trends
- Calculated Profit Margin %
- Created interactive dashboard filters and slicers

## Key Insights 
 - Different product categories contributed differently to overall sales and profit.
 - Regional performance varied across different regions.
 - Customer segments showed different levels of sales contribution.
 - Sales performance and profitability were analyzed together to understand actual business performance.
 - Monthly trends helped identify changes in sales and profit over time.

## Recommendations
 - Focus inventory and promotional activities on high-performing categories.
 - Investigate weaker-performing regions to identify opportunities for improvement.
 - Use customer-segment performance to develop targeted strategies.
 - Monitor Profit and Profit Margin along with Sales.
 - Use Monthly sales trends for better inventory and promotional planning.

  ## Dashboard 
  
  The Power BI Dashboard provides interactive views of:
  - Sales & Profit by Category
  - Sales & Profit by Region
  - Sales by Month
  - Sales by Customer Segment
  - Quantity and Profit Margi KPIs
